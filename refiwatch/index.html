FAQ

Do you predict rates?

No — and anyone who says they do is selling something. RefiWatch is a decision tool, not a crystal ball. It tells you exactly when the math works at any rate you enter; it never claims to know where rates are headed.

Where does my API key go?

It stays in this browser's localStorage. Every AI call goes straight from your browser to your provider. We never see it.

How accurate are the closing costs?

They're rule-based estimates (origination, appraisal, title, recording, prepaids). Your lender's Loan Estimate is the source of truth — always verify before you sign.

What does RefiWatch cost?

$9/month or $90/year after a 30-day free trial. One refinance at the right moment saves tens of thousands in interest — the subscription is a rounding error.
