FAQ

Do I need an API key?

For the AI-drafted action list, yes — any OpenAI, xAI, or Anthropic key works, and the deterministic tax math runs without one. Keys stay in your browser's local storage.

Is this tax advice?

No. Vestr produces educational estimates from the tax tables we ship with it. Tax law is complex and personal — run everything past a licensed CPA before acting.

Which equity types are supported?

RSUs, ISOs, and NSOs, with monthly or quarterly vesting, cliffs, and custom schedules. 83(b) elections and disqualifying dispositions are modeled as scenarios.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Grants, runs, and your API key live in this browser's localStorage. There is no Vestr server.

Bring your own key

Pick a provider, paste a key. It stays in this browser's local storage — it is never sent to our servers. Every AI call goes straight from your browser to the provider.

New to API keys? OpenAI keys · xAI console · Anthropic console. Set a small spend limit (e.g. $5) on your provider account.
