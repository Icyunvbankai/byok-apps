Questions

Where do transcripts come from?

You paste them in — free sources linked right in the app: Motley Fool transcripts, Seeking Alpha transcripts, and company investor-relations pages. Three steps: open the transcript, copy the full text, paste it here. We validate what you paste (and tell you if it looks like Q&A-only).

How much does a breakdown cost on my key?

Roughly $0.15–0.60 depending on transcript length and model — the app shows the exact token count and estimated cost after every breakdown. Long transcripts need big-context models; we default to Claude Sonnet for this reason.

Do you give buy/sell recommendations or price targets?

No — deliberately. We do comprehension, not prediction. You get what management said, what changed, what's risky, and both sides of the case, each tied to a quote. The call is yours.

What happens to my transcripts and data?

Everything lives in this browser's localStorage — your key, your watchlist, your quarter history. Nothing is sent to our servers. Export or delete it anytime in Settings.
