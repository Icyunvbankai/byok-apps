FAQ

Do I need an API key?
Yes — it's why the app is $14 instead of $14/month. The key costs you pennies per deal (about a cent), and it never leaves your browser.

Does it work without a key?
Yes. All the math — OTD price, payments, fee flags, lease-vs-buy — runs in code with no key. The key only powers the AI-written negotiation analysis.

Is my deal data uploaded anywhere?
No. Deals live in this browser's localStorage. Export or delete them anytime in API Key settings.

Bring your own key

Pick a provider, paste a key. It stays in this browser's local storage — it is never sent to our servers. Every AI call goes straight from your browser to the provider.
