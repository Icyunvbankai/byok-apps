FAQ

Do I need an API key?
Yes — the AI features (expense categorization, missed-deduction suggestions) run on your key. The Q&A, ledger, and savings math work without one.

Is this tax advice?
No. WriteOff organizes your expenses and estimates savings. A CPA makes the final call on what you claim.
