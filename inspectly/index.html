How BYOK pricing works

Inspectly runs on your OpenAI, xAI, or Anthropic key — pasted once, stored only in this browser, never on our servers. A full triage costs roughly $0.01–0.04 of your own API credit. We charge for the triage engine, not the tokens.

Bring your own key

Pick a provider, paste a key. It stays in this browser's local storage — it is never sent to our servers. Every AI call goes straight from your browser to the provider.

New to API keys? OpenAI keys · xAI console · Anthropic console · Google AI Studio. Set a small spend limit (e.g. $5–10/month) on your provider account while house-hunting.
Heads up: Anthropic's API sometimes blocks direct browser calls (CORS). If the key test fails below, OpenAI or xAI will work fine.
